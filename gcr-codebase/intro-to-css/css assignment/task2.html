Welcome to Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is popularly known as the Pink City because of its beautiful pink buildings.

History of Jaipur

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II. The city was carefully planned and became an important centre of art, culture and trade.

The architecture of Jaipur reflects a combination of traditional Rajasthani styles and scientific planning.

Famous Places

Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is one of Jaipur's most famous landmarks, known for its unique windows and beautiful architecture.

Amber Fort

Amber Fort is a historic fort famous for its grand architecture, courtyards and beautiful surroundings.

City Palace

City Palace is a historic complex containing palaces, courtyards and museums.

Culture of Jaipur

Jaipur is known for its colourful festivals, traditional handicrafts, folk music, dance and delicious Rajasthani food.
